Phantom Edges Guide: How the Secondary Weapons Work

Phantom Blade Zero splits its arsenal into two layers: your everyday combos come from your Blades, but what really raises your ceiling in combat is the Phantom Edges - a resource-limited secondary-weapon system you pull out at the moments that matter. Here's how the loadout works, the four weapon categories, and why skilled players actively swap Phantom Edges mid-fight instead of just mashing Blade combos.

Everything here about Phantom Edge categories and the equip system comes from official combat demos plus multiple outlets' coverage (reporting concentrated in the first half of 2026). Exact numbers (damage, ammo caps, cooldowns) haven't been officially published, and we're not stating any as fact - this page only covers what's been corroborated across multiple sources. We'll fill in the real numbers after launch (October 29, 2026).

The loadout: 2 Blades + 2 Phantom Edges

Soul can carry 2 primary Blades and 2 Phantom Edges at once, freely swapping between them mid-fight. Blades handle your everyday combo damage with no usage limit; Phantom Edges are the opposite - each one has a clear resource limit, and the confirmed arsenal is 30+ Blades against roughly 20-25 Phantom Edges, making this the larger, more situational half of the weapon system.

The 4-piece loadout you carry at once - Blades on the left with no usage cap, Phantom Edges on the right, resource-limited

The four categories

Four Phantom Edge categories are known so far, each with its own icon and combat role:

Might (axe icon)

Heavy melee-range Phantom Edges built for single-hit burst damage - public demos have shown weapons like giant cleavers and long-handled maces, and trailers have also shown heavier polearm/lance-type weapons in this vein.

Ranged (bow icon)

Noticeably longer reach than melee weapons, used to create distance or hit targets melee can't easily reach - coverage has mentioned bows and hand-cannon-type weapons in this category.

Technique (twin-sickle icon)

Unconventional weapons built around a distinct special move - the emphasis here is on what the move does functionally (breaking guard, controlling space) rather than raw numbers.

Arcane (bell icon)

The least-documented category so far - an early trailer showed a bell-type item functioning as a Phantom Edge, but the developers haven't detailed the mechanic yet. We'll fill this in after launch.

Combat role: why swap Phantom Edges mid-fight

Phantom Edges aren't "backup weapons" - they're tools for solving specific problems:

  • Burst windows: when an enemy is staggered or guard-broken, a Phantom Edge can land more damage in that window than a Blade combo would;
  • Situational answers: facing a ranged target, needing area damage, or hitting something your Blade just can't reach - swapping to the right Phantom Edge category solves it faster;
  • Offense and defense: some Phantom Edges also carry defensive utility, not just raw damage.

Tie this back to the Sha-Chi system: since Phantom Edges are resource-limited, you can't just spam them - they're best saved for the burst window right after a Ghoststep, or the moment Hellwalker's adaptive AI leaves an opening. That's exactly why experienced players treat Phantom Edge timing as seriously as reading Killer Moves and Brutal Moves.

Practical tip: Exact Phantom Edge numbers (ammo caps, cooldowns, damage multipliers) haven't been announced yet - once the game launches, try each category in low-stakes fights first to get a feel for its resource drain before settling on a fixed loadout. No category is strictly stronger than another; it's really about which specific problem you want it to solve.

FAQ

What are Phantom Edges?
Phantom Blade Zero's secondary-weapon system - resource-limited, used for burst damage or specific situations, complementing the unlimited-use Blades.

How many can I equip at once?
Public demos show 2 Phantom Edges alongside 2 Blades, freely swappable mid-combat.

How many Phantom Edge categories are there?
Four: Might, Ranged, Technique, and Arcane - Arcane has the least public detail so far.
