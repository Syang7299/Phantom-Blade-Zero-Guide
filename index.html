About this site: Phantom Blade Zero (影之刃零) is a third-person dark wuxia action-adventure built on Unreal Engine 5 by Beijing-based S-GAME, launching October 29, 2026 on PC (Steam) and PlayStation 5. The story is set in the Phantom World, a fictional setting blending Chinese wuxia, steampunk, and occult elements. Hong Kong action choreographer Kenji Tanigaki's team led the combat choreography, and Donnie Yen has served as producer since 2023 while also appearing as a cast member.
