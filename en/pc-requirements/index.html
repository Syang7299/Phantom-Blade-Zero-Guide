PC Requirements Explained

Phantom Blade Zero runs on Unreal Engine 5, and S-GAME has published minimum and recommended specs on its Steam store page. The minimum bar isn't especially high (a GTX 1660 will run it), but both tiers require upscaling to be enabled, and an SSD is mandatory.

This information is drawn from the Steam store page and multiple outlets, all reporting consistently in August 2026. Requirements could still be fine-tuned before launch (October 29, 2026) - treat the Steam store page as the final source of truth once the game ships.

A few easy-to-miss details

  • Even the minimum spec assumes upscaling. Hitting 1080p/30fps on a GTX 1660 leans on DLSS/FSR-class upscaling rather than native rendering - if your card doesn't support the relevant upscaler, actual performance may fall short of the stated numbers.
  • RAM is identical across both tiers, at 16GB. GPU and CPU are where the minimum and recommended specs actually diverge - if you're still on 8GB, plan on a RAM upgrade before anything else.
  • An SSD isn't a suggestion, it's a requirement. Both tiers explicitly call for solid-state storage. UE5 open-world games like this one tend to lean hard on read speed, and a mechanical drive risks slow loads and texture pop-in on top of it.
  • CPU requirements are fairly conservative. Both the i5-8700K and the Ryzen 5 3600 are mainstream CPUs from around 2018-2019, so the bottleneck for older rigs will mostly be on the GPU side.

Practical tip: If you're on a GTX 1660-class card, check ahead of time whether it supports DLSS or FSR (the GTX 16-series doesn't support DLSS, so you'd be relying on FSR or similar) - otherwise your real-world experience may land below the stated minimum numbers. If your budget allows it, targeting the recommended spec (RTX 3060 Ti-class) outright is the safer bet, since post-launch content and the harder difficulty modes (like 66 Days) are only going to demand more from your hardware, not less.

FAQ

What GPU do I need for minimum settings?
An NVIDIA GTX 1660 6GB or AMD RX 5500 XT 8GB, targeting 1080p/30fps with upscaling enabled.

What frame rate does the recommended spec hit?
An RTX 3060 Ti 8GB or RX 6700 XT 12GB-class card targets 1440p (2K) at 60fps.

Do I need an SSD?
Yes - both the minimum and recommended specs require one. No official mechanical-drive support path has been announced.
